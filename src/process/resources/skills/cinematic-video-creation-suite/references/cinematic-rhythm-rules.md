# Cinematic Rhythm Rules

## Scene-Level Rhythm
- Opening scene: establish world and tone before dense coverage.
- Mid scenes: increase shot density near conflict escalation.
- Climax: shorter durations + stronger motion contrast.
- Resolution: lower motion intensity, cleaner composition.

## Shot-Level Rhythm
- Setup shot -> action/detail shot -> reaction shot -> transition shot.
- Avoid repeating identical scale/movement for more than 3 consecutive shots unless stylistically intentional.
- Keep emotional beats readable: reaction coverage is mandatory after major plot action.

## Duration Hints
- Exposition: 4-6s
- Dialogue exchanges: 3-5s
- Action burst: 1-3s
- Emotional close-up hold: 5-8s

## Continuity of Motion
- Preserve movement vectors across cuts unless intentional disorientation is declared.
- Prefer motivated camera movement tied to character intent.

## Prompting Quality
Prompt should describe what can be filmed, not abstract emotion labels alone.
